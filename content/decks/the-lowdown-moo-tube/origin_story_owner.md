# Owner origin story: The Lowdown Moo Tube
Source: Andrew (owner), Showrunner chat, 2026-10-29 19:11 JST. Verbatim. Canonical input for the Chronicler; do not invent additional backstory.

The Lowdown Moo Tube comes from my love of unique or funny sounding words and game play mechanics. First off. Didgeridoo? Fantastic instrument in my opinion. UP there with Mongolian throat singing that produces a sounds that just burrows in your ear. But with cards like the Didgeridoo or Dingus staff, when i learn about them, i cant get them out of my head until i build something around it. I have also had a recent obsession with vintage cards and minotaur's have not received much of a major printing since the early days of magic. This gave me a unique opportunity to build two things simultaniously, a deck that gets to explore vintage cards and scratch that funky name itch but not being held back by the constraints of vintage only cards. And the name of the deck... A deck name in my opinion is nearly as important as the deck itself. I am tired of hearing about a bracket whatever mid range spell slinging deck or a proliferate deck... I want a named deck that tells you what it is in the most unique way possible. And the Lowdown Moo Tube is inspired by the jive names for other instruments like the "Licorice Stick".
